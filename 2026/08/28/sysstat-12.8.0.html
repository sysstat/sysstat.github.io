Sysstat 12.8.0

28 Aug 2026

This is a technical and debug version. Among the improvements, note that sysstat will now read the files in /proc only once per collection cycle. Also the addition of a new flag (-G) to iostat, cifsiostat and tapestat to display statistics in GiB/s, a fix regarding how pgsteal/s (displayed by sar -B) is calculated, and various other fixes and optimizations.
